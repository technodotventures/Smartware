# The canonical record's `state` is the writer's projection of `status` (ADR-0016)

## Intent

Close the correction-durability defect carded out of `t_3ba3ee39` (ADR-0014 → *Explicitly not decided
here*): a `correction` with `reason: 'wrong'` / `'extraction_error'` retires the claim in the derived
row but appends a canonical line that is still `state: 'active'`, so the retraction never reaches the
JSONL every rebuild reads. The card's ask was to **decide the `state` vs `status` precedence** (three
options given), implement it, and record the decision — not to patch the one call site.

**Out of scope:** the two published-pattern residuals on a replayed line (`/claim_id`, `/tombstone_id`,
carded `t_0b079fbf`), the demotion-pointer drop on the forgotten path (`t_8098b097`), and any repair
pass over existing pods' rows.

## What was done

- **Measured first, on the base tip (`eb83381`), with the deliverable test itself** — the test file is
  the probe: `claim_extracted` + `correction {reason:'wrong'}` replayed in one pass leaves the claim a
  two-line chain `v1 active` / `v2 active supersedes: 1`. RED: 5 failed | 2 passed (the two passing are
  the `changed`/`duplicate` regression guard and the tombstone-path guard).
- **Decided: `insertClaim` derives `state` from `claim.status`** (`statusToState`) — one field carries
  the caller's intent, and the row's `state` column and the record's active/forgotten branch are written
  from that single resolution. Three facts decided the direction: `claimVersionVals` already rebuilds
  `status` from a record's `state` (so a disagreeing pair describes a row that disagrees with its own
  canonical line — it is not rebuild-equivalent); `statusToState` is total and many-to-one while
  `stateToStatus` is not its inverse; and the only in-tree writer that ever set `state` explicitly
  (`updateClaimStatus`) sets it *from* `status` one line before `insertClaim`. Full record:
  `docs/adr/0014-canonical-state-is-a-projection-of-status.md`.
- **The card's option 2 was implemented and measured, not argued away.** Setting
  `original.state = statusToState('retracted')` in the two `replay.ts` branches does fix the retraction —
  and writes the *replacement* claim as a forgotten v1 record, because `buildCorrectedClaim` spreads the
  mutated claim: the user's correction silently disowns its own replacement. Raw output attached to the
  card (`option2-local-fix-counter-evidence.log`). Rejected, with the reason that rescuing it needs a
  second patch plus an ordering constraint nothing enforces.
- **One line of `src/` changed** (`src/layer1/store.ts`, `insertClaim`, plus its comment). No schema,
  protocol or spec text moves: `verify:schemas` still reports 31 files, `SHA256SUMS` untouched.
- **Deliverable test** `test/layer1/replay-correction-state.test.ts` (7 tests): the retraction is
  asserted on the raw JSONL line (`state: 'forgotten'`, `supersedes: version - 1`, no `content`,
  `tombstone_id`, the exact known error list), on the derived row (`status`/`state`/`t_invalidated`),
  on the recall-eligible sets (`getActiveClaims`, `isEffectiveCurrent`, `getEffectiveCurrentIds`), on a
  second `replayAll` pass, and on a rebuild from the canonical JSONL alone with the rows deleted
  (`deleteAllClaims` + `setDataDir`). `extraction_error` is asserted as the same shape; `changed` and
  `duplicate` are the other half of the rule (`state: 'active'` with `superseded_by` intact); the writer
  rule itself is pinned directly (a stale `state` losing to `status`, the mirror pair writing `active`,
  and `updateClaimStatus` still writing its forgotten line).

## Resulting state

- A correction that retires a claim (`wrong`, `extraction_error`) now writes `state: 'forgotten'` with
  `supersedes: version - 1` to the canonical line, once, and the row agrees with it — in the live run and
  after a rebuild from the JSONL alone. The replacement claim the correction spawned is untouched.
- The precedence is one rule rather than a per-call-site workaround: `status` is the input, `state` is
  the projection, and any caller that flips `status` on a claim read from the store writes the matching
  record on its next insert. `changed` / `duplicate` are unchanged (measured before and after).
- ADR-0016 is a **Proposed** record. Branch `fix/l1-replay-correction-state` (off
  `wip/smarty/l1-forgotten-supersedes` @ `1c75fd9` — the lane's ADR-refile tip, not the `eb83381` it was
  measured on), local only — not pushed, nothing merged. It rides
  ADR-0014's merge gate: the parent branch already moves published-schema bytes, so the owner's approval
  is the gate for this lane as a whole.
