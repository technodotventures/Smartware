# P1-3: host-facing health contract, lane-explicit counts, Coffee-trial SLOs

## Intent

Make a company brain **operable** by the host that runs it: one machine-readable
report that answers lease state, brain-open state, compile queue depth/age,
ingestion cursor lag, lane-explicit counts, drift, denied access, retention/forget
receipts, storage size, backup freshness, recall/write latency, recovery events —
and a Coffee-trial SLO verdict over those numbers that can say "not proven".

Also in scope: **correct the misleading `layer3.indexed` metric** (it counted one
of three FTS lanes while reading as "everything is indexed").

Out of scope: changing the five-verb protocol surface, schemas, the lease's
arbiter (ADR-0007 keeps ownership external), and any host-side dashboard.

## Resulting state

- A host can poll `health` and alert on `slo.status === 'breach'`, on any `drift`
  record, and on `role === 'observer'` for a process that believes it is the
  writer. Nothing in the report can carry tenant content (asserted on the
  serialized report).
- `STATUS.layer3.indexed` no longer exists anywhere; the four lanes are named.
- SLO verdicts are three-state: an unmeasured objective is `unknown`, so a fresh
  trial is never `ok`. Thresholds live in `COFFEE_TRIAL_SLO` and the doc together.
- The metrics store is deletable operational state: a wipe loses history, not
  memory, and the report says so by absence.

## Unresolved

- `drift` covers two projections (`wiki_manifest`, `observation_fts`) — a
  detector, not a consistency proof for every index. Extending the list is
  additive and expected.
- Latency quantiles are bucket-resolution **upper bounds** (largest edge 10 s,
  overflow reported as `null`); a future exporter should keep the histogram
  rather than re-deriving quantiles.
- `stale_writer_refusals` legitimately moves during an ownership takeover; the
  doc says so, but no host-side "in takeover" signal exists yet, so an operator
  may still chase a benign refusal.
- SLO thresholds are trial policy (1 s recall p95, 500 ms write p95, 15 min
  queue age, 5 min ingestion lag, 24 h backup, 20-sample latency minimum) — they
  are not measured against a real Coffee workload yet; the first trial should
  re-baseline them, and that is `t_ba868906`'s adapter work plus trial data.
- Next bounded action: wire the Coffee reference adapter (`t_ba868906`) to poll
  `health` and render `slo` + `ingestion` in the host UI.
